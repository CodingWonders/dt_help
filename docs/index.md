# Welcome to DISMTools

!!! note "Infinity Documentation Preview"

	DISMTools 0.8 introduces a visual refresh of the documentation, with a new design and layout. This is just a preview of the new design, and we will continue to improve it in future preview releases.

	All the pages are still available in the sidebar on the left. However, you will also find a table of contents when navigating to a page on the right. You will also find new functionality for **searching** the documentation, even with suggestions. Finally, click on an image to view it in full size.

	We hope you enjoy the new design and layout of the documentation, and we look forward to your feedback!
